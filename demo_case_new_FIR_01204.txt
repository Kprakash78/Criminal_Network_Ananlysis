Investigating Officer's Preliminary Notes:

registered to an office space in Vaishali Nagar, Jaipur.

Call Detail Records for mobile number +91-9845123456 (registered to
Vikram Oberoi — the same number linked to the historical Shakti
Enterprises case) show an unusual spike of 61 outgoing/incoming calls
between 19:30 hrs and 21:00 hrs on 04/09/2026, immediately after several
victims attempted to contact him demanding refunds.

A recurring associate, Manish Oberoi, previously linked to vehicle
RJ14EF9988 in the historical case, was again seen using the same vehicle
outside the Vaishali Nagar office on two occasions this week.

Bank account records linked to account number XXXXXX4432, registered
under "Shakti Growth Fund" at a private bank branch in Jaipur, show total
inflow of approximately Rs. 34,00,000 across 22 separate victim deposits
between 25/08/2026 and 04/09/2026, followed by a sudden outward transfer
of Rs. 31,50,000 to an account associated with Vikram Oberoi personally
on 04/09/2026 — the same day the phone stopped being answered.

A separate line of inquiry identified the "Shakti Growth Fund" office as
being managed on the accounting side by a contract accountant, Anita
Deshpande, operating out of the same Vaishali Nagar office address
previously flagged in Case FIR/2026/00905 in connection with account
XXXXXX2211 and a suspected money-mule structuring pattern. Anita
Deshpande's current account activity for "Shakti Growth Fund" shows a
similar pattern of multiple smaller deposits consolidated into larger
outgoing transfers, consistent with the structuring behavior previously
flagged in the historical case. Investigators note this may indicate
Anita Deshpande's services were reused across two unrelated-seeming
schemes, or that the "unnamed client" from the 2026 case and Vikram
Oberoi are connected.
