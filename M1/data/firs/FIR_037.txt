FIRST INFORMATION REPORT
FIR No.: FIR_2026_00037
Date: 07/03/1996
Police Station: Qutub Colonnade, New Delhi
Reporting Officer: Inspector Suresh Dahiya
Case Reference: JESSICA_LAL_1999

INCIDENT REPORT:
On the night of 29-30 April 1999, a social gathering/bar event was being held at Tamarind Court Restaurant, Qutub Colonnade Complex, New Delhi. At approximately 02:00 hours, when the bar stopped serving alcohol, a verbal altercation occurred.

PRIMARY ACCUSED:
1. Manu Sharma (full name: Siddharth Vashisht), approximately 24 years old, son of Congress MP Vinod Sharma. Resident of Chandigarh and New Delhi. Primary shooter.
2. Amardeep Singh Gill, associate of Manu Sharma, present at the scene.
3. Vikas Yadav, son of a senior UP politician, present at the scene.

VICTIM:
Jessica Lal, female, approximately 34 years old. Working as a celebrity barmaid at the event. Shot in the head at close range. Declared dead at AIIMS Hospital, New Delhi.

WITNESSES:
1. Shayan Munshi — key eyewitness (later turned hostile).
2. Malini Ramani — present at the scene.
3. Karan Rajput — security personnel.

BALLISTIC EVIDENCE:
Two empty .22 caliber cartridge cases recovered from the scene.

REGISTERED SECTIONS:
- IPC Section 302 (murder)
- IPC Section 201 (causing disappearance of evidence)
- IPC Section 120B (criminal conspiracy)

INVESTIGATION NOTE:
Multiple witnesses turned hostile during trial. Delhi High Court acquitted accused in 2006. Public outrage and media campaign led to reinvestigation. Delhi High Court convicted Manu Sharma in 2006 following retrial ordered by Supreme Court. Supreme Court confirmed life imprisonment in 2010.

All findings require investigator verification before any action is taken.
